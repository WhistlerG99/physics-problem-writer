%+++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++
%+++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++
%+++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++
%+++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++
The type of corrections to the maxwell equations we will investigate is
%+++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++
\begin{gather}
\nabla_{\mu}F^{\mu\nu}
+
\xi^{2}
R^{\mu\nu}_{\,\,\,\,\,\,\, \sigma \tau}
\nabla_{\mu}

\begin{gather}

\frac{M}{r^{3}}

\left.
-
