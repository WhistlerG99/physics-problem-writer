Problem Context:
Consider a scalar field in a Schwarzschild background using stereographic coordinates. The scalar field $\Phi(r, t)$ in this curved spacetime satisfies the equation:

$$\partial^{2}_{t} \Phi - q(r) \partial_{r}(p(r) \partial_{r} \Phi) = -\frac{l(l+1)}{r^{2}} q(r) H(r) \Phi$$

where $q(r) = 1 - \frac{2M}{r}$, $p(r) = r^2$, and $H(r)$ is a function to be determined. Assume the potential

Solution:
$$\Phi(r,t) = A(r) e^{i \phi(r) - i \omega t} Y_{l}^{m}(\theta, \varphi)$$

where $A(r)$ is the amplitude, $\phi(r)$ is the phase, $\omega$ is the frequency, and $Y_{l}^{m}(\theta, \varphi)$ are spherical harmonics. For simplicity, consider the WKB approximation for large $r$. 

Given that the absorption cross section $\sigma_{abs}$ for the scalar field in the Schwarzschild background is:

$$\sigma_{abs} = \frac{\pi}{2 \omega^2}(\sqrt{1 + 108 \omega^2 M^2} -1 + 54 \omega^2 M^2)$$

Assuming that the leading order term in the expansion of $\sigma_{abs}$ for small $\omega M$ is $54 \pi M^2$, calculate the critical angular momentum $l_c$ for which the transmission amplitude $T_l(\omega)$ transitions from 1 to 0.

Question:
What is the critical angular momentum $l_c$ rounded to the nearest integer?

SOLUTION:
The absorption cross section $\sigma_{abs}$ is given by:

$$\sigma_{abs} = \frac{\pi}{2 \omega^2}(\sqrt{1 + 108 \omega^2 M^2} -1 + 54 \omega^2 M^2)$$

For small $\omega M$, the leading order term is $54 \pi M^2$. This corresponds to the classical capture cross section for a point particle in a Schwarzschild background, which is related to the impact parameter $b_c$ by:

$$\sigma_{abs} \approx \pi b_c^2$$

So we have:

$$\pi b_c^2 = 54 \pi M^2 \implies b_c^2 = 54 M^2 \implies b_c = \sqrt{54} M$$

The critical angular momentum $l_c$ is related to the impact parameter by:

$$b_c = \frac{l_c}{\omega}$$

Thus, equating $b_c$ from both expressions:

$$\frac{l_c}{\omega} = \sqrt{54} M \implies l_c = \omega \sqrt{54} M$$

For the transmission amplitude $T_l(\omega)$ to transition from 1 to 0 at $l = l_c$, we assume $\omega M \ll 1$ and choose $\omega = 1/(2M)$ (as a typical frequency scale in such problems), then:

$$l_c = \frac{1}{2M} \sqrt{54} M = \frac{\sqrt{54}}{2}$$

Calculating $\sqrt{54}/2$ gives approximately $3.6742$. 

Rounding to the nearest integer:

$$l_c \approx 4$$

Answer   : 4
